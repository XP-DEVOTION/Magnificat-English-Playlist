My soul magnifies the Lord and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior. He has looked with love on His humble servant.

The Mighty One has done great things for me and holy is His name. 
He has mercy on those who fear Him in every generation. He has shown the strength of His arm. 
He has scattered the proud in their selfish hearts. 
He has cast down the mighty from their thrones and has lifted up the lowly. 
He has filled the hungry with good things, and the rich He has sent away empty. 
He has come to the help of His servant Israel, for He has remembered His promise of mercy,
