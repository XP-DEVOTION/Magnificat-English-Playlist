In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. 
Let's take a quiet moment together to pause, to breathe, and to pray. 
Today we'll reflect on the Magnificat, Mary's beautiful song of praise. 
As we journey through this month of June, dedicated to the Sacred Heart of Jesus, may her words draw us closer to God's mercy and love. 

Let's begin. 
My soul magnifies the Lord and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior. 
He has looked with kindness on His lowly servant. 
From now on all generations will call me blessed. 
The Mighty One has done great things for me and holy is His name. 
His mercy is for those who fear Him from generation to generation. 
He has shown the strength of His arm. He has scattered the proud in their pride. 
He has brought down the powerful from their thrones and lifted up the lowly. 
He has filled the hungry with good things and sent the rich away empty. 
He has helped His servant Israel, remembering His mercy, just as He promised to our ancestors, 
to Abraham and his children, forever.

May these words echo within us, a gentle reminder of God's faithfulness and His great mercy. 
In this month dedicated to the Sacred Heart, may we carry praise, gratitude and trust deep within our hearts. 
Let us return to this prayer throughout the days of June. 
May it guide our steps and bring light to our hearts. 
Amen.
