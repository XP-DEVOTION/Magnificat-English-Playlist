Welcome to the Monthly Magnificat: Prayer and Reflection.
This audio is designed for daily use—
a simple way to enter into prayer,
to rest in God’s Word,
and to let the rhythm of the Magnificat
shape the rhythm of your month.

As we begin, take a moment to breathe,
to slow down,
and to let your heart settle in the presence of God.

Each month, we turn to Mary’s song
and allow her words to guide our hearts
into deeper trust, deeper peace,
and a deeper communion with Christ.

Today, we continue our journey through the month of January—
the month that opens a new year of grace
and invites us, day after day,
to rest in the power and peace
of the Holy Name of Jesus.


And now, let us pray.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.
Amen.

January is the month the Church dedicates
to the Holy Name of Jesus—
a Name spoken by angels,
a Name adored by saints,
a Name that brings mercy, healing, and peace
to every soul who calls upon it.

And so, as we move through this month,
we pause,
we breathe,
and we place His Name at the center of our lives.

O Holy Name of Jesus,
written in heaven and whispered in love,
be the first word on our lips this year.


With that prayer resting in our hearts,
we turn now to Mary’s canticle of praise.

Let us pray the Magnificat.

My soul magnifies the Lord,  
and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior,  
because He has noticed the humility of His servant.

For look, from now on all generations will call me blessed,  
because He who is mighty has done great things for me,  
and holy is His name.

His mercy extends from generation to generation  
to those who fear Him.

He has shown strength with His arm;  
He has scattered the proud in the thoughts of their hearts.

He has brought down the mighty from their thrones  
and lifted up the humble.

He has filled the hungry with good things  
and sent the rich away empty.

He has helped Israel His servant,  
remembering His mercy,  
as He promised our ancestors,  
to Abraham and his descendants forever.

And together we pray:

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit,
as it was in the beginning, is now, and will be forever.
Amen.


As this new year unfolds,
we ask Jesus to write His Name
over every moment that awaits us—
the joys,
the challenges,
the quiet days,
and the unexpected ones.

And as we look ahead,
we remember that His Name
is not just something we speak—
it is a place we rest.

His Name is our peace.
His Name is our strength.
His Name is our hope.

So as you move through this month,
carry His Name with you.
Let it steady your heart,
guide your choices,
and remind you that you are never alone.

May the Holy Name of Jesus
be the light that guides us,
the shield that protects us,
and the love that carries us
through this month and through this year.

And so we pray once more:

O Holy Name of Jesus,
be the first word on our lips this year.
Come, Lord Jesus.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.
Amen.


Thank you for praying the Magnificat with us today.
May Mary’s song continue to echo in your heart,
guiding you into deeper peace,
deeper trust,
and a deeper love for Christ.

As you go forward,
may the Lord bless you and keep you,
and may His grace accompany you
through every moment of this day.

We look forward to praying with you again
as we continue this journey of faith,
month by month,
and day by day.

Until then,
may God be praised,
and may His Mother intercede for you.
